# ADR-005: Gas sensor selection

## Decision
MQ-2 gas sensor is selected initially for the gas node in the SHOS system as a prototype and for testing purposes. The MQ-2 sensor is a low-cost, widely available gas sensor that can detect a range of gases, including LPG, smoke, alcohol, propane, hydrogen, methane, and carbon monoxide. It is suitable for the initial development and testing of the gas node in the SHOS system.

which means that in the future, if the MQ-2 sensor does not meet the requirements of the SHOS system, it can be replaced with a more suitable gas sensor that meets the specific needs of the system.

putting SGP40 in mind for future development, as it is a more advanced gas sensor that can detect a wider range of gases and has better accuracy and sensitivity than the MQ-2 sensor. However, the SGP40 sensor is more expensive and may not be necessary for the initial development and testing of the gas node in the SHOS system.

## Status
Accepted
